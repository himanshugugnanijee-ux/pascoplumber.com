HVAC Services

Heating and cooling installation, repair, and maintenance to keep your home comfortable through every Washington season.

Washington's climate asks a lot of a home's heating and cooling system — cold, damp winters east and west of the mountains, and summers that increasingly bring real heat. Pasco Plumbing provides HVAC installation, repair, and maintenance across the state, applying the same approach we bring to plumbing: honest diagnosis, clear explanations, and a free estimate before any work begins.

Whether it's a furnace that won't hold a consistent temperature, an air conditioning system that's stopped cooling effectively, or a home that's ready for a new heating and cooling system entirely, we walk through what's actually happening with your equipment and what your realistic options are — not a one-size-fits-all recommendation.

Signs You Need HVAC Services

  • Uneven heating or cooling between rooms in the same house
  • A system that runs constantly without reaching the set temperature
  • Unusual noises — banging, squealing, or rattling — from the furnace or AC unit
  • A noticeable spike in energy bills without a change in usage
  • Weak airflow from vents throughout the house
  • A system that's short-cycling, turning on and off frequently
  • Strange odors when the heating or cooling system runs
  • A system that's 12-15+ years old and hasn't had recent maintenance

Heating & Cooling Repair

HVAC problems often have several possible causes behind the same symptom — a system blowing warm air instead of cold could be a refrigerant issue, a failing compressor, or something as simple as a dirty filter restricting airflow. We diagnose based on what's actually happening in your specific system rather than assuming the most expensive cause, and explain what we find before recommending a repair.

For furnaces, common issues include ignition problems, a failing blower motor, thermostat miscommunication, or a clogged filter restricting airflow enough to trip a safety switch. For air conditioning and heat pump systems, low refrigerant, a failing capacitor, or a dirty condenser coil are frequent culprits. We carry the diagnostic approach to figure out which one applies to you, rather than replacing parts by trial and error.

New System Installation

When a heating or cooling system has reached the end of its useful life, or a home simply needs a new system installed, sizing matters more than most homeowners realize. A unit that's too large cycles on and off too frequently, wearing out components faster and creating uneven temperatures. A unit that's too small runs constantly and still struggles to keep up. We size new systems based on your home's actual square footage, insulation, and layout rather than defaulting to whatever was there before.

We also talk through efficiency ratings and their real-world impact on your energy bills, since a slightly higher upfront cost on a more efficient system often pays for itself over several years of Washington winters and summers. You'll get a clear, free estimate that reflects the specific system and installation your home needs.

Maintenance That Prevents Bigger Problems

Regular HVAC maintenance — filter changes, coil cleaning, checking refrigerant levels, inspecting electrical connections, and testing system cycling — catches small issues while they're still inexpensive to fix. A system that's properly maintained also tends to run more efficiently, which shows up directly in lower energy bills over a Washington heating or cooling season. We can talk through a maintenance schedule that fits your system and how heavily it's used.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should my HVAC system be serviced?

We generally recommend a maintenance check once or twice a year — typically before the heating season and before the cooling season — to catch small issues before they become expensive repairs.

How do I know if I need a repair or a new system?

It depends on the age of your system, the frequency of past repairs, and the specific issue. Systems well past 12-15 years old with recurring problems are often better replaced than repeatedly repaired, and we'll walk you through the real numbers.

Do you work on both furnaces and air conditioning systems?

Yes, we handle heating and cooling systems, including furnaces, air conditioners, and heat pumps.

Why is my energy bill so much higher than usual?

A sudden increase often points to a system working harder than it should — a dirty filter, low refrigerant, or a failing component are common causes worth having checked.

Is HVAC repair available 24/7?

Yes, we provide emergency HVAC service, since a heating failure in winter or a cooling failure during a heat wave can't always wait.

Do you install smart thermostats?

Yes, we install smart and programmable thermostats as part of new system installs or as standalone upgrades to an existing system.

How much does HVAC repair typically cost?

It depends on the specific issue and system type. You'll get a free, clear estimate before any repair work begins.
